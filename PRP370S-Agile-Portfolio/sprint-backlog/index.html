1. Introduction

The Sprint Backlog is a collection of tasks selected by the team to work on during a sprint. It helps the team focus on the activities that need to be completed within a particular development period.

For Pulse Guard, the sprint activities included planning and designing the mobile application interface, organising tasks and contributing to the overall project.

2. Sprint Goal

The goal of the sprint was to contribute towards developing the Pulse Guard application by working on assigned project tasks and improving the user interface and user experience.

6. Challenges and Improvements

One important part of working in a group project was making sure that the different parts of the application could work together. As the Mobile UI/UX Designer, I needed to consider how the mobile screens would support the overall application.

In future sprints, I would improve my planning by breaking design activities into smaller tasks, keeping track of progress and communicating design updates with the rest of the team.

7. Personal Reflection

The Sprint Backlog helped me understand how Agile teams organise their work into smaller activities. It showed me that having a clear sprint goal makes it easier to focus on the tasks that need attention.

My contribution focused on the mobile UI/UX design of Pulse Guard. Using Figma allowed me to work on the visual structure of the application, while Trello helped me follow the tasks assigned to me. I learned that managing time, communicating with team members and reviewing progress are all important parts of working in an Agile team.
