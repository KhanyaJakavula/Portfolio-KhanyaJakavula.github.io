My Reflection

What I learned from the Agile Team Charter

Creating and understanding the Agile Team Charter helped me recognise the importance of having clear goals, responsibilities and expectations within a team.

I learned that communication and teamwork play an important role in keeping a project organised. Having agreed responsibilities also helps team members understand what is expected of them.

Through this process, I developed a better understanding of how an Agile team can work together and remain focused on its project objectives.
