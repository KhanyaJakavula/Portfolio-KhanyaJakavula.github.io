1. Project Overview

Pulse Guard is a crime alert application concept designed to help users report crime incidents and stay informed about safety concerns in their communities. The project focuses on using digital technology to improve awareness and make crime-related information more accessible.

2. Project Purpose

The purpose of Pulse Guard is to provide a platform where users can report incidents and receive relevant safety information. The project aims to support community awareness through features such as crime reporting, location-based information and crime alerts.

3. Project Objectives

  • Design a user-friendly crime alert application.
  • Make it easier for users to report crime incidents.
  • Support awareness of crime in different areas.
  • Use location-based information to improve user awareness.
  • Work together as a team using Agile principles.

5. Team Values

  • Teamwork: Supporting one another and working towards a shared goal.
  • Respect: Listening to everyone's ideas and opinions.
  • Communication: Sharing updates and discussing challenges.
  • Responsibility: Completing assigned tasks and contributing to the project.
  • Continuous improvement: Learning from feedback and improving our work.

6. Tools and Technologies

  • Figma: Used to design the mobile application screens and user interface.
  • Trello: Used to organise, manage and track project tasks.

7. Agile Working Approach

The team used an Agile approach to organise the project into manageable tasks. Trello helped us keep track of activities and monitor progress. Team members worked on their assigned responsibilities and contributed towards the overall project.

8. Communication and Collaboration

Communication and collaboration were important throughout the project. Team members needed to share progress, discuss tasks and communicate when challenges came up. This helped us coordinate the different parts of the application.

9. My Role and Contribution

My role in the Pulse Guard project was Mobile UI/UX Designer. I was responsible for designing the mobile application screens using Figma and focusing on the overall user experience. I worked on the mobile interface, including the layout, visual elements and structure of the different screens. My aim was to make the application simple to understand and easy for users to navigate.

I also used Trello to manage and track project tasks. This helped me keep track of assigned activities, monitor progress and understand which tasks still needed attention. Through this experience, I was able to apply my UI/UX design skills while also learning more about Agile project management, task organisation and teamwork.

10. Team Agreement

As a team, we aimed to work together, respect each other's contributions and take responsibility for our assigned tasks. We understood that communication, cooperation and completing work on time were important to the success of the Pulse Guard project.

11. Personal Reflection

Working on Pulse Guard helped me understand how important teamwork and planning are in an Agile project. I learned that designing an application is not only about making screens look good, but also about understanding the users and making the application easy to use.

Using Figma allowed me to practise my UI/UX design skills, while Trello helped me organise and follow my tasks. One lesson I took from this experience is that team communication is important because different parts of an application need to work together. Overall, the project helped me improve my design, planning and collaboration skills.
